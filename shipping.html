Shipping & delivery

Last updated: 21 November 2026

We pack every order by hand at our kitchen in Konaseema, Andhra Pradesh, and send it to you as quickly as we can.

Where we deliver

We currently deliver to the pincodes accepted at checkout. If your pincode is not accepted, message us on WhatsApp — we are adding new areas regularly.

Fresh paneer is delivered only to nearby pincodes, packed chilled, because it is a fresh product.

Dispatch and delivery time

Orders are dispatched within 1–2 working days after we confirm your payment. Delivery usually takes 1–3 days within Andhra Pradesh and Telangana, and longer for other locations. Delays caused by courier partners, holidays or weather are outside our control, but we will keep you informed.

Delivery charges

The delivery charge (if any) is shown in your cart before you pay. Orders above the free-delivery amount shown in the cart are delivered free.

Tracking

When your order is shipped we update its status and share the courier tracking number on the order tracking page and on WhatsApp.

Damaged parcels

If your parcel arrives damaged or leaking, please take photos or a short unboxing video and message us within 24 hours of delivery. See our returns & refunds policy.
