Privacy policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

This policy explains what information SML Foods (Seetha Maha Lakshmi Foods) collects when you use this website and how we use it.

What we collect

  • Your name, mobile number, email (optional) and delivery address when you place an order.
  • Your order details and the UPI transaction reference you enter.
  • Your cart and checkout details are saved in your own browser so you don't have to type them again.

We do not collect or store your UPI PIN, card or bank account details. Payments happen inside your own UPI app.

How we use it

Only to process, deliver and support your order, and to contact you about it (for example on WhatsApp). We do not sell or rent your information.

Who we share it with

We share your name, phone number and address with our courier or delivery partner only to deliver your order. Order data is stored securely using Google Firebase.

Your choices

You can ask us to correct or delete your information at any time by contacting us. We keep order records as long as needed for accounting and legal requirements.
