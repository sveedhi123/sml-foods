Our story

Seetha Maha Lakshmi Foods

సీత మహాలక్ష్మి

We are a family food business from Tatipaka in the Konaseema region of the Godavari delta. Our ghee, pickles and paneer have been made and sold in our own shop, and now we bring the same products to your home.

Everything is made by us, in small batches, using the recipes our customers keep coming back for.

  • Our own products

    Made in our kitchen at Tatipaka — never repacked from someone else.

  • Small batches

    Made in small quantities so every jar you receive is fresh.

  • Andhra recipes

    Avakaya, gongura, magaya, dabbakaya — the way they are made at home.

  • Packed with care

    Sealed, leak-proof packing for safe delivery.
