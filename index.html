Our Sacred Process

Three Generations. One Unbroken Method.

Every jar carries the blessing of tradition — no shortcuts, no machines, no compromise.

01

Cultured Curd Churning (Bilona)

Fresh A2 Gir cow milk is boiled, cooled, and cultured overnight. The set curd is hand-churned bidirectionally at dawn using a wooden bilona — separating golden butterfat from buttermilk, preserving every nutrient.

4–5 AM churning 30L milk → 1kg ghee
02

Sun-Cured Regional Spices

Andhra's finest mangoes, gongura leaves, and tomatoes are hand-cut and layered with cold-pressed mustard oil, rock salt, and stone-ground spices. Sun-cured on cotton cloth for 21 days — developing depth no oven can replicate.

21 days sun-curing Zero vinegar
03

Hygienic Small-Batch Packing

Each batch is poured into sterilized glass jars and ceramic containers by hand. Nitrogen-flushed, tamper-sealed, and labeled with the churn date. No plastic, no bulk tanks — just pure tradition sealed fresh for your kitchen.

Tamper-proof seal Glass & ceramic only

Why Choose SML

Purity You Can Taste. Heritage You Can Trust.

Four pillars that define every product leaving our kitchen.

A2 Gir Cow Milk

Sourced exclusively from free-grazing Gir cows. A2 beta-casein protein is easier to digest and richer in nutrients than conventional A1 milk.

Cold-Pressed Oils

Our pickles use only kachi ghani mustard oil — cold-pressed at low temperatures to retain natural antioxidants, flavor, and therapeutic properties.

No Artificial Colors

Zero synthetic dyes, preservatives, or flavor enhancers. The vibrant hues come purely from turmeric, red chili, and the natural pigments of sun-ripened produce.

Farm-to-Kitchen Freshness

Churned, cured, and packed in micro-batches weekly. Every jar carries its production date — so you know exactly how fresh your food is.
