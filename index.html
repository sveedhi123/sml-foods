Our home

Born in the coconut groves of Konaseema

గోదావరి ఒడ్డున, కోనసీమ పచ్చదనంలో

Konaseema — the green delta where the Godavari meets the sea — is where Seetha Maha Lakshmi Foods began. Our ghee, pickles and paneer have been made and sold in our own shop at Tatipaka for years. Now we bring the same jars, made the same way, to your home.

Avakaya in summer, dabbakaya and gongura the way Godavari families make them, and slow-cooked ghee with its grainy texture and rich aroma.
