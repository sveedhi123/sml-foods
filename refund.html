Returns, refunds & cancellations

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Because our products are food, we cannot accept returns of opened or used items. We will always make it right if something goes wrong with your order.

Damaged, leaking or wrong items

If a jar arrives broken or leaking, or you received the wrong item, message us on WhatsApp within 24 hours of delivery (within 12 hours for fresh paneer) with your order number and photos. After checking, we will send a replacement or give a full refund for the affected item.

Cancellations

You can cancel your order for a full refund any time before it is dispatched. Message us on WhatsApp with your order number. Once an order is shipped it cannot be cancelled.

Payment issues

If we cannot find your UPI payment for an order, we will contact you. Orders without a matching payment are cancelled. If money was deducted but the order could not be fulfilled, we refund the full amount.

How refunds are paid

Refunds are sent back to the UPI account you paid from, usually within 5–7 working days of approval.
